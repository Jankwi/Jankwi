## Hi there, I'm Jankwi 👋
I'm a CS student at the University of Warsaw, and a big math nerd. My interests lie at the intersection of Research and Engineering.
Recently I've done projects in Machine Learning, as well as in Algorithmics. Take a look:

## My Projects
* [RCRL](https://github.com/Jankwi/RCRL) - Conditioning Contrastive Reinforcement Learning on abstract goals. Accepted to NeurIPS 2026 workshops.
* [Breaking the Sorting Barrier in Code](https://github.com/Jankwi/SSSP-Code) - Implementation and analysis of the BMSSP algorithm, famously "beating" Dijikstra.
* [Blockudoku RL](https://github.com/Jankwi/Blockudoku-RL) - Library for Reinforcement Learning for the game of blockudoku + succesfull experiment examples.
* [Takuzu](https://github.com/Jankwi/Takuzu) - Fast (CPU) torch C++ solvers for Takuzu. Used for trajectory generation for RCRL.

## My Contributions
* [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) - Expanding the scope of multi-modal UUIDs. Reducing latency through UUID-based cache lookups.
* [NVIDIA Dynamo](https://github.com/ai-dynamo/dynamo) - Minor changes, made during my internship at NVIDIA
* [The SIO2 project](https://github.com/sio2project/oioioi) - For one year, I was part of a group of 10 students, working on platforms supporting the Polish Olympiad in Informatics.
